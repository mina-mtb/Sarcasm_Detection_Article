  % --- INTRODUCTION (SHORTENED & SHARPENED) ---
  \begin{alertblock}{Introduction}
    Sarcasm is a pragmatic phenomenon whereby intended meaning opposes the literal utterance. In social media, reliable detection depends on \key{conversational context}, prior discourse, and shared assumptions. Classification of isolated comments is brittle and often fails to capture pragmatic nuance.
    
    This work evaluates whether parent-comment context and retrieved labeled evidence improve binary sarcasm detection on Reddit. We hypothesize that context provides critical incongruity signals, while retrieval enhances robustness through precedent-based evidence.
  \end{alertblock}

  % --- DATA & TASK ---
  \begin{block}{Data \& Task}
    Binary classification of Reddit replies as sarcastic or non-sarcastic using a balanced held-out split from the SARC dataset \cite{khodak2018large} (Table~\ref{tab:data}). The dataset is partitioned into 70\% training, 15\% validation, and 15\% test.

Our architecture integrates a transformer classifier with a calibrated retrieval stacker, as depicted in Figure~\ref{fig:pipeline}.   \vspace{-0.45cm}
  \begin{block}{Experimental Setup}
    \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}L{0.2\linewidth}Y@{}}
      \textbf{TF-IDF} & 1--2 grams, 100k features, balanced logistic regression. \\
      \textbf{Transformer} & \texttt{distilroberta-base}, 256 tokens, batch size 8, 3 epochs. \\
      \textbf{Training} & Learning rate \(2\times10^{-5}\), weight decay 0.01, AdamW via Hugging Face Trainer, best checkpoint by validation F1. \\
      \textbf{Retrieval} & BGE-base embeddings, cosine/inner-product search, top-\(k=5\), up to 50k indexed train rows. \\
      \textbf{Stacker} & Logistic regression over model probabilities and neighbor features; threshold 0.56 calibrated for accuracy. \\
    \end{tabularx}
  \end{block}

  % --- DISCUSSION (SHORTENED) ---
  \begin{block}{Discussion}
    No single model dominates all metrics. The \key{retrieval stacker} emphasizes precision/AUC with more conservative sarcasm predictions. The \key{context-aware transformer} achieves higher recall/F1, identifying more sarcastic replies at lower precision.
    
    \textbf{Why context helps:} Sarcasm often arises from incongruity between a reply and its parent context \cite{wallace2014humans,riloff2013sarcasm}. The transformer model detects this contrast, improving recall from 0.803 to 0.816.
    
    \textbf{Why retrieval helps:} Retrieval favors conservative predictions (.809 precision, .871 AUC); report split: .791 Acc., .810 Prec., .785 F1 (\(n=75{,}748\)).
  \end{block}
  \vspace{0.2cm}

  % --- CONCLUSION (POLISHED) ---
  \begin{alertblock}{Conclusion}
    Context-aware modeling maximizes recall when sensitivity to sarcasm is the priority. Retrieval-augmented stacking delivers the best overall accuracy and precision for applications that require balanced ranking performance. Future work should investigate longer conversational histories, external knowledge sources, and stronger retrieval architectures to address failure cases that require world knowledge.
  \end{alertblock}
